# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want `daily-kural-js` to be a welcoming project. We pledge to make participation free from harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and patient in issues and reviews.
- Accepting criticism of the code, and giving it that way too.
- Focusing on what is best for the people who use the library.
- Showing empathy.

Examples of behaviour that is not accepted:

- Insults, sexual language or images, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, or public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Other conduct which would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour through a private GitHub security advisory on this repository, or by contacting the maintainer Abilash outside the public issue tracker. Reports are reviewed and kept as private as possible.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits, or issues, and may block someone from the project, if their behaviour breaks this code.
